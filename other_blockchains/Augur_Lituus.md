A new version of Augur was created. So, here is my review of their design.

https://github.com/AugurProject/whitepaper/blob/master/Lituus/English/Augur_Lituus_Whitepaper.pdf Here is their white paper. I am looking now.

They interviewed me when they were designing this, and it is nearly a copy of Amoveo. Kind of disappointing that they decided to mention all those other projects and not Amoveo.

So, their idea is that when the fork occurs, the attacker would need to buy up and lose <1/2 of the votecoins. So, they can inflate the supply of votecoins, auction off the new coins, and use the proceeds to make all the bets whole.
So, they allow for bets priced in ETH, and they have this complicated mechanism to hopefully recover the ETH if Augur should fork.

What I like about this, in comparison to the old Augur design, is that votecoin holders aren't compelled to vote so often. They only need to weigh in during a forking event. Which will probably happen never, or almost never.

But, this still has the limitation that the total value of bets at any one moment needs to be lower than the market cap of Rep (their votecoins).
So, the trading fees need to be higher than the interest rate applied to the market cap of Rep.
So, it will go into the same death-spiral as Truthcoin and the old version of Augur. Fees just cant be high enough to sustain the market cap of Rep at any configuration. So the price wont stop falling.
