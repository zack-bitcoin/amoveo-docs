Comparing Blockchain Land projects
=========================

What does a blockchain land registry need to win?

1) The ability to draw lines on the map to put your land plot anywhere you want it. Moving every fence and road in the world to conform to some unflexible database is too expensive. The database needs to be able to put the land plots in the places where they already exist.

2) A way to prevent land speculation. Otherwise land crisis will cause the project to fail.
