Why Pay the Land Tax
===============

The kind of people rich enough to invest in cryptocurrency tend to live in places where the land registry works as intended, and so they just assume that the land registry works correctly everywhere.

The reality is that land registries don't work in most places.
They are only cost effective if the land plots are very big, or very expensive. Low-valued and small land plots are more costly to maintain in the registry in comparison to the tax revenue that can be generated from them. So, most people do not get any land registry service.

The land that lacks a clear owner lies empty, or gets exploited to have it's trees clear-cut, or to be used to dump trash. 

People need to live somewhere, so they live on land that they don't have a clear title for. They are forced to invest in the land to build their home. They seek any way to build up evidence that they own the land, because they don't want to be kicked out of their home. They go out of their way to participate in alternative land registries. It is not unusual for the same neighborhood to have 2 or 3 parallel land registries.

In some neighborhoods, everyone is expected to memorize everyone else's land. If there is a legal dispute, you bring in neighbors as witnesses to say who's land is who's. 
In other places, the neighborhood church maintains paper copies of who's land is who's. In a legal dispute you can bring in the minister as a witness. Sometimes people burn down these churches as a way to steal land.

Once the Amoveo land registry exists, it will be an immutable trustless record. You can prove to a court of law exactly how long you have been using the land, and what the land is worth to you.
