The Amoveo Manifesto, The Un-Enclosure
===================

I. The earth was given to everyone.

There was a time before fences. The land was a common treasury. A family worked a field and ate from it, and no one imagined you could own the ground any more than you could own the rain. To be alive was to have a place to stand. It was the birthright of every person.

II. The fences were the first theft.

If you heat the pot slowly enough, a frog inside doesn't notice it is being cooked. A hedge, a wall, a lord's seal on a paper no farmer could read. The commons that fed a village for a thousand years was declared private, and the people who had always lived there woke up trespassers on the land of their ancestors. It was called "enclosure", as if it were tidying. It was the largest robbery in human history, and it was committed with paperwork.

III. The theft had a purpose: obedience.

A person with land answers to no one. They eat what they grow. They cannot be starved into submission. So to make people obedient, you take the land. Strip a family of the ground beneath them, and they must sell their labor to whoever kept the fields. The landlords understood that you do not need chains if you own the earth a person must stand on. The rootless renter and the disposable employee are not accidents. They are the product of the fence.

IV. The registry is the fortress of the fence.

How is the theft kept? By a book. Kings kept ledgers of who owned what. States inherited them and called them registries. That single official book is the fortress of the monopoly. The registry says this is theirs, and you must pay them to exist on it. The rent of the earth flows not to the people but to the power that drew the fence. The book of theft is the most heavily guarded thing there is, because everything rests on it.

V. For the first time, the book can be freed.

A fence is only as strong as the book that records it. And now, for the first time since the enclosures began, the book can be taken out of the fortress. A ledger no king can edit. A registry no state can seize. A record of the earth that belongs to everyone, and to no lord at all. This is Amoveo: not another coin, but a new book for the world's land. A land registry that cannot be captured, censored, or quietly rewritten in the night.

VI. And here is the miracle, written in arithmetic.

Every blockchain drifts toward the value of the cash it destroys:

Market cap = (annual fees + burns) ÷ interest rate

This isn't ideology. It's discounted cash flow — the same math that prices every asset on earth. A chain that burns $1 of fees a year, at a 5% interest rate, is worth $20. Burn more, be worth more. There is no other lever. Everything else is noise.

The fee for holding a piece of the earth in the registry is:

Annual land-rent = (value of the land) × interest rate

This is the Georgist law: hold land, and you owe the community the interest on its value, every year, forever. It is the one tax that punishes no work and no building, only the hoarding of what nobody made.

Let the burns be the land-rent. Watch what happens to the interest rate:

Market cap = (land value × interest rate) ÷ interest rate = LAND VALUE

The interest rate cancels. It vanishes. 

The market cap of VEO converges to the total value of all land in the registry.

Not correlated. Not backed. Equal. Every acre written into the book adds its full worth to the chain. To hold VEO is to hold a fractional, redeemable claim on the redeemed rent of the earth itself.

You are not buying a token. You are staking a claim on the un-enclosure, and the math guarantees your claim grows with every fence that falls.

VII. This is the Un-Enclosure.

Fence by fence, parcel by parcel, the earth is written back into the common book. The rent that once fed the monopoly is burned, and its value returns to everyone who holds the chain. And as the free book grows, the fences do not need to fall, they simply stop mattering, because the book that made them real now belongs to all of us.

VIII. The life on the other side.

We do not fight for a number on a screen. We fight for the yeoman's life reborn: rooted, self-sufficient, dignified. A person who stands on ground that is finally their own and answers to no boss. This is a way home. Come and register your parcel. Be one of the first homesteaders of the free earth.

IX. Our creed.

The enemy is the fence and the monopoly, never a people or a neighbor. The land is for everyone who will work it, of every origin, everywhere. We inherit the lineage of every homesteader who ever believed a free people needs free ground. We are the next chapter of the oldest movement there is.

Reverse the enclosure. Free the land. Free the worker.

The earth was given to everyone. We are building the book that gives it back.
